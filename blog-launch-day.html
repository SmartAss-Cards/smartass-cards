Launch Day

Victoria Xiong-Gnandt · March 20, 2025

March 20 isn't just the first day of spring 🌷 — it's also the official launch of Smartass Cards, your new go-to for sympathy and loss e-cards that ditch the clichés and bring real, human connection (with a side of dark humor).

But that's not the only milestone. It also happens to be our 16th wedding anniversary 🤵🏻👰🏻 — because nothing says "commitment" like nearly two decades of love, laughter, and inside jokes. If we've learned anything in that time, it's that humor is one of the best ways to get through life's hardest moments. That belief is exactly why Smartass Cards exists.

So, whether you're celebrating, grieving, or just trying to figure out how to support someone going through a tough time, we've got a card for that. And since they're e-cards, you can send one instantly — no waiting, no postage, just a message guaranteed to make someone laugh when it matters most.

Join us in celebrating March 20 — for the launch, for love, and for the moments that deserve more than a generic "thoughts and prayers" card.
